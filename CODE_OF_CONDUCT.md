## Our Standard

Everyone interacting in this repository — issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other communication channel — is expected to act professionally and respectfully.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive in all discussion, especially disagreement over a technical decision.
- Give and accept clear, evidence-based feedback; prefer a reproducible example over a general claim.
- Focus criticism on the code, the design, or the argument — never on the person.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope, architecture, and the published [decisions](docs/decisions/DECISIONS_INDEX.md) and [standards](docs/php-engineering-standards/STANDARDS_MANIFEST.md) governing this repository.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private communication related to this project.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive, off-topic, or bad-faith contributions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces owned by this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review) and in any other venue where an individual is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to **support@maatify.dev** — the same contact published in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are reviewed and handled at Maatify's discretion, which may include a warning, a request to edit or remove content, or removal from the project's spaces.
